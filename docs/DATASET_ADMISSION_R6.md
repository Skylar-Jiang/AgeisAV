# Qualified dataset admission — R6 proposal

This policy separates label confidence from independence. It does not change admission-v1 history, production assets, thresholds or fusion. The user authorized operational/proxy exploratory work and explicitly declined independent signoff. This document itself admits no strict validation/final sample.

## Automated exploration is a separate lane

Official source/package/member SHA, signatures, available exact/near/training audits are automated evidence checks. They do not require a human to repeat verifiable mechanics. Freeze the scope, policy and source assumptions; record operational/proxy labels; then run bounded exploratory comparisons without per-file approval. Report observed control-positive signals, abstentions and cost, not strict independent Recall/FPR or human-reviewed truth. Record prediction observation permanently so files cannot later become an unseen evaluation set.

R6's five software release groups contain358automatically audited files. Before first prediction, a separate source-group split assigns260(.NET SDK,CMake,fd)to operational exploration and retains98from two other project/release groupsunscored for future independent admission. two sealed candidatesremain sealed source-held candidates; R5quarantine and old malicious development roles are unchanged. Exploration authorization is not strict admission and no human labels are fabricated.

## Roles and labels

1. **Development:** exact raw SHA/PE integrity plus primary technical behavior evidence, or checksum-bound official publisher provenance. Automated preparation is identified. Observed detector scores, error-analysis files and policy-selection examples stay development.
2. **Qualified validation:** human-approved label basis and explicit scope authorization, unobserved files, known exact/near/source audits, grouped frozen manifest and policies before scoring. Unknown complete upstream training membership remains disclosed residual risk, rather than an invented `training_overlap_cleared=true`. Known overlap/observation is still a blocker.
3. **Sealed final candidate:** source/time objectives and identities reserved before prediction. A reserved candidate is not admitted final data. Final use additionally needs label review, source/time and residual-risk acceptance, frozen selected policy and explicit release. Validation feedback must not select the final population.

Labels: malicious / presumed benign under authorized use / uncertain. Family attribution is separate. A MalwareBazaar tag, detector vote or valid signature alone supplies no truth. Dual-use/PUP, certificate-only IOCs, decoys, legitimate sideload hosts, failed downloads and parser errors are retained explicitly, outside the primary truth denominator.

## Practical human review

Provide 5–15 representative cases with exact SHA, raw verification evidence, publisher/technical source, locator, recommendation and unresolved questions. No malicious content is exposed. Record reviewer, timezone-aware date, decision and reason only from an actual human reply.

Separate **label acceptance** from **evaluation-use authorization**. For official packages a human may explicitly authorize a checksum-bound source/release group and its complete enumerated member manifest after reviewing examples and package evidence. This is source-group review, not individual reverse engineering or per-file human inspection. Approval of one example never implicitly approves a whole package or future files. The human can approve individuals only, reject a group, or leave uncertain.

The private ten-case packet and358identity freeze remain historical preparation. The user did **not** approve their strict validation or provide malicious label signatures. Human fields stay blank. A separate steering record captures operational-experiment authorization only. The260observed exploration identities will be excluded from future unseen validation even if human labels are later accepted. Future strict review should concentrate on unresolved evidence and one explicit scoped choice; automated mechanics should not become repeated approval work.

## Independence, grouping and temporal limits

Keep separate audit fields for known training membership, prior observations/reference identities, global available fuzzy graph, campaign/family, publisher/project/release/shared component and time evidence. Bind inventory digests and missing coverage. Full author training and near-training inventories may be unavailable; report this explicitly and limit the claim to independence on documented known evidence. Such a cohort cannot establish model-wide novelty.

Build the available TLSH<=40 / SSDeep>=80 graph over new **and prior** identities, including prior/prior bridges. These are conservative screening edges, not proof of semantic or statistical independence. Keep linked groups together. Preserve project/release/package parent units even when fingerprints differ. Reviewed campaign/report relationships constrain malicious splits; unknown membership does not become independent singleton evidence.

Known observed release siblings stay development. A known publisher alone is a higher-level dependence/coverage factor, not proof that every unrelated new binary leaked. Publish both project/release and publisher strata. Source-held final claims require holding the relevant publisher/project or research source out prospectively, rather than renaming development after seeing scores.

Publication, acquisition, COFF timestamp, signing timestamp and repository first_seen are different facts. Provider first_seen and release version cannot prove a binary's first global appearance. Preserve verified source dates, do not rewrite earlier inputs, and never claim temporal novelty from an untrusted header timestamp.

## Predefined comparison

Freeze general LightGBM 0.5; existing Win64 0.9831222501035356 with general Win32 fallback; the five engine versions and positive OR. Keep applicability failures/abstentions explicit. A graded-evidence research policy can distinguish reference/rule/Clam evidence-positive, similarity/ML-only review, no-positive undetermined and execution failure abstention. Freeze exact category semantics/mappings before scoring; no arbitrary weighted pseudo-probability or signature-based unconditional allow rule.

Validation only may select future versioned policies. Compare paired rates, disagreements, review workload, abstentions, per-family/campaign macro recall, architecture and installer/SFX/software categories. Report all-input and completed denominators, conservative malicious recall treating abstention as missed, and cost. Precision/F1 require the cohort class mix and are not deployment estimates.

Use exact file-rate confidence intervals with an IID caveat, plus paired parent/component bootstrap where enough genuinely separate groups exist. Five software parent groups and hundreds of correlated SDK assemblies cannot establish extreme low FPR. At zero false positives, the one-sided 95% IID upper bound is `1 - 0.05**(1/n)`; FPR<=0.1% needs at least 2,995 independent draws plus adequate population/source justification. Small qualified validation can support a named descriptive comparison without satisfying that target. If malicious admission or benign precision is insufficient, keep formal metrics unavailable and production frozen.

## Basis

The policy is a project proposal, not external certification. Relevant primary methodology: [TESSERACT](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity19/presentation/pendlebury) on temporal/population bias; [EMBER2024 authors](https://github.com/FutureComputing4AI/EMBER2024) on chronological data; [Datasheets for Datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010) on composition/uses/limits; [NIST proportion intervals](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm). Private source and human decision records remain outside the public repository.
